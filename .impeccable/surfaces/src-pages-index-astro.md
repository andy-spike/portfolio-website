Scope: the sheet at `/` and `/es/` — the index that must convert a reader into an email.

Visitor mode: Persuade. The hiring manager decides and acts here; the case studies are Read.

Audience and job: a technical hiring manager, three to five minutes, one tab away from GitHub, comparing several candidates in one sitting. Secondary: a recruiter who needs a forwardable file inside twenty seconds.

Action: write an email. Résumé download is the fallback for the recruiter, never the headline.

Proof: three public repos, an MCP server, an AppImage release, and one Terminal Cast. No metrics, no testimonials, no employment history — none exist.

Constraints: never state or imply seniority. Accent marks only what the reader can act on. English and Spanish, Spanish falling back rather than 404ing.

Direction and memorable moment: the showing carries numbered callouts on three of its own words, and the key beneath answers each with a fact the reader can verify. The claim is annotated into evidence inside the first viewport, before anyone scrolls.
