Scope: the arrival at `/` and `/es/` — the sheet a hiring manager lands on, and the one surface that must convert a reader into an approach.

Visitor mode: Persuade. The hiring manager decides and acts here; the case studies are Read.

Audience and job: a technical hiring manager, three to five minutes, one tab away from GitHub, comparing several candidates in one sitting. Secondary: a recruiter who needs a forwardable file inside twenty seconds.

Action: open the source on GitHub — the solid-ink block, the loudest action on the page. The résumé and email travel beside it as chips, for the recruiter and the reply.

Proof: three public repos, an MCP server, an AppImage release, and one Terminal Cast per project. No metrics, no testimonials, no employment history — none exist.

Constraints: never state or imply seniority. English and Spanish, Spanish falling back rather than 404ing. The reserved slots — portrait, the three Terminal Casts, résumé — stay in place and stay hatched; supplying a file is a content change with no layout movement.

Direction and memorable moment: classic web neo-brutalism, user-pinned, light only (see DESIGN.md). The first viewport is one printed block: the claim at up to 6rem with the emphasis word in a yellow highlight block, the solid-ink GitHub button, the rotated portrait window. The memorable moment is the press — every actionable block lifts off the paper on hover and presses flush on active, and the three colour-chipped project tickets and directory entries fill with their project colour as they lift.

Unresolved: whether the portrait, once supplied, stays in the hero brick or moves to the colophon; the six case-study bodies are owner-authored stubs (TO WRITE / POR ESCRIBIR) and remain the outstanding content work.
